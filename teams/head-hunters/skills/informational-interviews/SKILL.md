# Informational Interviews (Coffee Chats)

## Purpose
Learn how the role, company and local market really work, and build a relationship. Never ask for
a job in the chat; referrals come later from trust. Target 2–3 chats per week in an active search.

## Prepare (20 minutes)
- Read their LinkedIn, recent posts, company news, product and any public talks.
- Write a 30-second intro: who you are, the one-line Brazil context, what you're exploring.
- Pick 5–6 questions (below) you couldn't Google. Have a pen/notes ready; join video calls 2 min early.

## Question bank (pick by goal)
Path & role: "How did you move into product at [Co]?" · "What does a typical week look like?"
Company: "How are product decisions made — who has the final call?" · "What's the team focused on
this year?" · "What makes PMs successful here?"
Market: "How is the Toronto fintech market for PMs right now?" · "Which companies hire PMs from
abroad successfully?"
Newcomer-specific: "What gaps do you see when international PMs interview here?" · "How would you
frame my experience at [Brazilian co] for Canadian hiring managers?"
Close: "Is there anyone else you'd suggest I speak with?" · "May I keep you posted on my search?"

## Run it (15–20 minutes, keep to time)
Thank them → 30-second intro → their story first (listen, follow up on what's useful) → your
questions → ask for one more contact → offer something back (LatAm insight, an article) → end on
time. Take notes in the contacts sheet straight after.

## Follow up
- Within 24 h: thank-you referencing something specific they said and what you'll do with it.
- Act on their advice and tell them (2–4 weeks later): "I followed your tip about X — it worked."
- Light touch every 6–8 weeks (news, congratulations, a useful link).
- Referral: only once there's rapport and a specific role that fits. Make it easy: link, 3-line
  fit summary, your résumé. Accept "no" gracefully.

## Don'ts
Asking for a job · arriving unprepared · monologuing about yourself · going over time · generic
questions · no follow-up · asking them to review your whole résumé unprompted.

## Output
Prep sheet (bio notes, intro, chosen questions), live notes template, thank-you draft, next-touch
date logged in the tracker.
