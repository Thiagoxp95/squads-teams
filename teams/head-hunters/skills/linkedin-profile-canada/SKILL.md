# LinkedIn Profile for the Canadian Market

## Settings first
- Location: the target Canadian city (e.g. "Toronto, Ontario") once you are there or relocating
  imminently with authorization; recruiters filter by location.
- Open to Work: recruiters-only visibility; list target titles (Product Manager, Senior Product
  Manager, Product Owner), locations and "Remote".
- Custom URL (linkedin.com/in/firstname-lastname); professional photo is normal on LinkedIn
  (unlike the résumé); banner can show domain or city.
- Profile language English; optional secondary Portuguese profile.

## Headline (≤ 220 characters) = role + value + keywords
"Senior Product Manager · Fintech & Payments · Scaled Pix checkout to 30M users · Toronto · Open work permit"
Include work status in the headline or About only if it removes doubt (PR, open work permit).

## About (first person, 3–5 short paragraphs)
1. Hook (the 2 lines before "see more"): a specific result, not "passionate professional".
2. Proof: 2–3 outcomes with numbers; give Brazilian companies a one-line context.
3. How you work: discovery, data, cross-functional leadership — in Canadian/US PM vocabulary
   (OKRs, roadmap, A/B tests, PRDs, stakeholder alignment).
4. Keywords line: domains, methods, tools (Jira, Amplitude, SQL, Figma).
5. CTA: "Open to PM roles in Toronto or remote-Canada; work authorized (PR). Reach me at …".

## Experience
- Titles translated to the closest North American equivalent with honest scope
  ("Coordenador de Produto" → "Product Lead" only if you led product people; else "Senior PM").
- 3–5 achievement bullets per recent role (action → impact → metric), slightly warmer than a CV.
- Add media: a case-study PDF, a launch post, a talk.

## Skills & proof
10–15 skills ordered by the target role (pin top 3: Product Management, Product Strategy, your
domain). Ask 2–3 former managers/peers for recommendations in English that mention outcomes.
Certifications only if relevant (e.g. CSPO, Pragmatic, Reforge).

## Activity (2×/week, 15 minutes)
Comment thoughtfully on posts by target-company PMs and Canadian product leaders; post monthly
about a lesson learned. Visibility leads to inbound messages and warm intros.

## Checks
- [ ] Headline beyond the title, with keywords + location
- [ ] Hook before the fold; first-person About ending in a CTA
- [ ] Keywords consistent across headline, About, skills and the résumé
- [ ] Every Brazilian employer has context; titles honest
- [ ] Dates and titles match the résumé exactly (recruiters cross-check)
