# STAR Story Bank

Canadian interviews lean heavily on behavioural questions ("Tell me about a time when…") and want
real, specific examples, not hypotheticals. Prepare 6–8 stories, not 40 answers.

## Competency grid (each story covers 2–4; each competency needs 2+ stories)
| Competency | The question behind the questions |
|---|---|
| Ownership | Did you act beyond your lane when it mattered? |
| Conflict / disagreement | Can you disagree without burning the bridge — and commit when you lose? |
| Failure | Do you know why it failed and what you changed afterwards? |
| Ambiguity | What did you do when nobody could tell you what to do? |
| Influence without authority | Can you move engineers, design, sales, execs? |
| Prioritization under constraint | What did you cut and how did you choose? |
| Customer/data-driven decisions | How did evidence change your mind? |
| Growth | What's genuinely different about you since two years ago? |
Add PM-specific: launching a product, killing a feature, stakeholder management, metrics definition.

## Story format (STAR, 90–120 seconds spoken)
- Situation (1–2 sentences): context, with one-line company context for Brazilian employers.
- Task: your specific responsibility.
- Action (60% of the time): what *I* did — decisions, trade-offs, how I brought people along.
  Use "I" for your actions, "we" for team results.
- Result: a number with its denominator (from what to what, over what period) + what you learned.
Prepare a 10-second headline version and a 2-minute version of each.

## Selection rules
- ≥ 4 stories from the last 3 years.
- The failure story must be a real failure with real cost, and the changed behaviour should be
  visible in another story.
- Every story has at least one number you can explain ("How did you measure that?").
- Avoid stories that only work with Brazil-specific knowledge; explain Pix, CLT, etc. briefly.

## Mining stories
Walk the résumé bullet by bullet: "What was hard? Who disagreed? What would have happened without
you? What went wrong?" Capture raw notes, then shape into STAR. Never invent or inflate.

## Practice
Rehearse first sentences, not paragraphs. Do mock rounds with follow-up probes: "What would you do
differently?", "What did the engineer say?", "How did you know it worked?". Time answers; cut
Situation if it runs over 20 seconds. Practise aloud in English until fluent; it's fine to pause
and think.

## Output
Story bank table: story title · STAR notes · competencies covered · metric + denominator ·
10-second headline · likely questions it answers.
