# Canadian Résumé (Product Manager)

## Canadian conventions
- 2 pages max (1 for < 5 years); reverse-chronological; recent 10–15 years in detail.
- Exclude: photo, date of birth/age, marital status, gender, nationality, religion, height/weight,
  SIN, CPF/RG, full street address, reasons for leaving, references (provide on request,
  separate sheet). Hobbies only if job-relevant. (Job Bank guidance; human-rights norms.)
- Contact line: name · city, province · phone (Canadian number if possible) · professional email ·
  LinkedIn URL. Optional: "Work authorization: Permanent Resident" / "Open Work Permit (valid
  to MM/YYYY)" — include when it removes doubt.
- No first-person pronouns; Canadian spelling is acceptable (or consistent US spelling).
- Format: US Letter, single column, standard headings (Summary, Experience, Skills, Education),
  no tables/text boxes/icons/headers-footers; .docx or text-based PDF.

## Structure
1. Summary (2–3 lines, ≤ 80 words): target title + years + domain + strongest proof.
2. Experience: Title, Company — City, Country · MMM YYYY – MMM YYYY.
   One-line company context for non-Canadian employers ("Brazil's largest food-delivery app,
   40M users"). 3–5 bullets for recent roles, fewer for older ones.
3. Skills: grouped (Product: discovery, roadmapping, A/B testing, OKRs · Tools: Jira, Amplitude,
   SQL, Figma · Languages: English (fluent), Portuguese (native), Spanish).
4. Education & certifications with equivalents (e.g. "Bacharelado" → Bachelor's;
   "Pós-graduação lato sensu" → Post-graduate certificate; WES ECA only if a posting asks).
5. Optional: Volunteer / Canadian community involvement (shows local engagement).

## Bullets
Action verb → what you did → measurable outcome:
"Launched Pix instant checkout, raising conversion 18% → 24% and adding ~R$40M (CAD $10M) GMV/yr."
Convert currencies to CAD (approximate, labelled). Use the candidate's real numbers; if a number is
missing, describe the outcome, or give a conservative estimate marked "(confirm)". Never invent.
Show PM competencies across bullets: discovery, prioritization, delivery, data, stakeholders,
leadership.

## Converting a Brazilian CV
Remove photo/CPF/age/marital status · translate titles to honest North American equivalents
("Analista de Produto Sênior" → "Senior Product Analyst" or "Product Manager" only if the scope
was PM) · cut long course lists · replace duties ("responsável por") with outcomes · English only.

## ATS tailoring (per role)
Pull 10–15 keywords from the posting (titles, domains, methods, tools); mirror exact phrasing in
summary, skills and bullets naturally; keep the target job title in the summary. Save as
`Lastname-CV-<company>-<date>` and log it in the tracker.

## Checks
- [ ] ≤ 2 pages, single column, standard headings
- [ ] No excluded personal details; contact line complete
- [ ] Every recent bullet is an outcome with a metric or clear result
- [ ] Keywords from the posting appear naturally
- [ ] Dates/titles match LinkedIn; no unmarked assumptions
