# LinkedIn Outreach (Canada)

## Who to contact (warmest first)
1. Former colleagues/classmates now in Canada; Brazilian PMs in Canada (search "Brazil" in past
   companies/schools + "product manager" + city); alumni of your university.
2. PMs and product leaders at target companies (2nd-degree first; ask for intros).
3. People met at meetups (ProductTank, Product Camp, Brazil-Canada Chamber, TRIEC/ACCES events).
4. Tech recruiters and in-house talent partners at target companies.

## Canadian etiquette
Polite, concise, low-pressure; first names are normal. Self-promotion is fine when it is
specific and factual. Respect time: ask for 15–20 minutes, offer flexibility, thank people.
Never ask a stranger for a job or referral in the first message. One follow-up only.

## Templates (personalise the bracketed hook — never send generic)
Connection request (≤ 200 characters):
"Hi Ana — I'm a PM moving from São Paulo fintech to Toronto and enjoyed your post on [topic].
Would love to connect."
Advice ask after they accept:
"Thanks for connecting, Ana! I led payments product at [Co] (Brazil's [context]) and I'm now
exploring PM roles in Toronto fintech. Would you be open to a 15-minute chat about how your team
approaches [specific thing]? Happy to work around your schedule."
Referral ask (only after a conversation or warm link):
"[Name], thanks again for the chat. I've applied for the Senior PM role on [team] ([link]); it
matches my [specific experience]. If you're comfortable, would you refer me? I've attached a
short summary to make it easy."
Recruiter:
"Hi [Name] — I'm a Senior PM (8 yrs, payments/B2B SaaS), now in Toronto with an open work
permit. I saw [role]; my [specific result] maps to [requirement]. Could we set up a quick call?"
Follow-up (after ~7 days, once): "Hi [Name], just bumping this in case it got buried — no worries
if now isn't a good time."

## Make it give, not just take
Share a relevant article or insight, congratulate on a launch, offer Brazil/LatAm market
perspective, introduce two people who should meet.

## Volume and pacing
5–10 personalised messages/day max; LinkedIn throttles invitations (stay well under weekly
caps). Log everything in the job-pipeline-tracker contacts sheet.

## Checks
- [ ] Specific hook about them (not a template line)
- [ ] One small, clear ask; ≤ 120 words (≤ 200 characters for invites)
- [ ] States work status only when relevant (recruiters)
- [ ] One follow-up planned, date logged
- [ ] Candidate approves before anything is sent
