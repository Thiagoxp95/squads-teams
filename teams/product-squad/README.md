# Product squad

A full product team. You are the Product Manager.

The person is this squad's Product Manager: they set the goals, the priorities and the final call on scope. Paula (Product Owner) turns their goals into a ready backlog and runs the squad. Tomás (Tech Lead) owns the technical plan and code quality. Felipe and Bruno build, Diana designs, Quinn tests. Anything that needs deeper or independent testing goes to the QA team through Tomás or Paula. Nothing ships, merges to main, or goes to users without the person's OK.

## Members

- **Paula**, Product Owner (Lead): Turns the PM's goals into a prioritized, ready backlog and runs the squad. Skills: `user-stories-and-acceptance-criteria`, `backlog-refinement-and-sprint-planning`, `story-test-design`.
- **Tomás**, Tech Lead: Owns the technical design, breaks stories into tasks, reviews code. Skills: `technical-design-and-adrs`, `code-review`, `systematic-debugging`.
- **Felipe**, Frontend Developer: Builds the UI: components, state, accessibility, tests. Skills: `test-driven-development`, `systematic-debugging`, `ui-design-and-design-systems`.
- **Bruno**, Backend Developer: Builds APIs, data and integrations, with tests. Skills: `test-driven-development`, `systematic-debugging`, `unit-and-integration-test-design`.
- **Diana**, Product Designer: UX research, flows, UI design and accessibility. Skills: `ux-research-and-usability-testing`, `ui-design-and-design-systems`, `web-accessibility-audit`.
- **Quinn**, QA Engineer: Tests every story against its acceptance criteria before it ships. Skills: `story-test-design`, `bug-report`, `test-driven-development`.

## What you get

- Goals turned into a ready, prioritized backlog.
- Stories designed, built, reviewed and tested before you see them.

Every bot starts on Ask. Skills from a shared team arrive switched off; switch them on in each bot's Skills. Paula is the team's Lead.
